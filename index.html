StarPuzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android game StarPuzzle (package com.yond.StarPuzzle, "the game"), published by yondGames ("we", "us"). You can contact us at glazunov.jp@gmail.com.

1. What the game stores on your device

The game has no accounts or sign-in. Your progress (completed levels, stars and best results, coins, unlocked sphere sets, your chosen colour, your sound setting and your ad choice) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it. If you uninstall the game, this data is deleted with it.

2. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads (full-screen ads between some levels). Ads are provided by Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies. To show and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect technical data from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device model and operating system, language, app diagnostics, information about how ads are shown and interacted with, and information about in-app purchases (used to measure and improve advertising).

The first time you open the game we ask whether ads may be personalised using your advertising ID:

  • Accept: ads can be personalised.
  • Decline: you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

You can change your choice at any time in the shop (shield button). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Unity's own privacy information: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

If you buy "Remove ads", the game stops showing ads.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs and "Remove ads") are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives confirmation that a purchase was completed so it can give you the item. The purchase library from Unity that the game uses may also send identifiers, purchase information, crash reports and diagnostics to Unity. Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Services used by the game

  • Unity Ads and Unity services (Unity Technologies): advertising and purchase handling, as described above.
  • Google Play and Google Play services (Google): installing the game, in-app purchases and the advertising ID.

These providers collect and use data under their own privacy policies, linked above. We do not receive the data they collect about you.

5. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for or use your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any account details, and we do not run our own servers that store personal data about you.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or below the minimum age that applies in your country).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, California or Japan) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to personalised advertising and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you, the practical steps are: change your ad choice in the game, reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, and uninstall the game to remove local data. You can ask Unity about the data it holds using the contact options in Unity's privacy policy (linked above). For other requests, email glazunov.jp@gmail.com.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, where required, ask for your choice again.
